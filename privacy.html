Privacy Policy — Selman Publishing Tool

Last updated: November 19, 2026

“Selman Publishing Tool” is a personal tool that runs only on its owner's own computer. It is used solely by its owner to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channel and Instagram account. It is not offered to other users.

Data accessed

  • YouTube Data API: video upload (youtube.upload) and reading the channel name (youtube.readonly) only.
  • Instagram API (via Facebook Login): reading the linked Facebook Page and Instagram account names, and publishing content only.

Storage and sharing

Access tokens and video metadata are stored only on the owner's computer. They are never sent to any server, third party, advertising or analytics service, and are never sold or shared. Videos and captions are sent directly to YouTube and Instagram only.

Google API usage

This tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. This tool uses YouTube API Services: YouTube Terms of Service · Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time via Google account permissions and Facebook › Settings › Business integrations. Locally stored tokens are fully deleted by deleting the tool's data folder.
